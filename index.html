QR Wallet OB - Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 10, 2026

QR Wallet OB is a QR code wallet application developed by Oktay Babir.

Information Storage

QR codes and related information are stored locally on the user's device.

Camera and Photo Library

The app uses the camera to scan QR codes and accesses selected photos to import QR codes.

Data Collection

The developer does not collect personal information through the app.

Data Sharing

The app does not automatically transmit saved QR codes to the developer. Users may choose to export or share their own information.

Data Deletion

Users can delete saved QR codes within the app. Removing the app may also remove locally stored data.

Changes

This policy may be updated if the app's features or data practices change.
